## Sources specific to ISO certifications (verify against certifying body where possible)

| Standard | What it signals | Verification approach |
|---|---|---|
| ISO 9001 (Quality Management) | Baseline operational discipline. Low individual signal but relevant as part of a pattern. | Search certifying body register (DNV, Nemko, Kiwa, BSI, Bureau Veritas) or company's quality page. |
| ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) | Formal environmental management system, often paired with or replacing Miljøfyrtårn for larger companies. Score input for Mission and Purpose. | Same as ISO 9001. Cross-check with annual report sustainability section. |
| ISO 45001 (Occupational Health and Safety) | Direct signal for Work Environment. Indicates documented HSE system and management commitment. | Same as ISO 9001. |
| ISO 27001 (Information Security) | Maturity signal for Career Development and Work Environment, especially for technical, financial and healthcare roles. | Same as ISO 9001. UK has the IASME register at https://iasme.co.uk |
| ISO 27701 (Privacy Information Management) | GDPR-aligned management system, extension of 27001. Direct signal for governance maturity. | Same as ISO 9001. |
| ISO 42001 (AI Management System) | Published December 2023. Strong signal for AI Maturity Posture. Few certified organizations yet, so even pursuit or roadmap commitment is meaningful. | Verify with named certifying body. Cross-check with annual report and AI policy page. |
| EcoVadis rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) | Sustainability rating widely used by procurement. Companies often publish medal level. | Look for medal badge on company sustainability page. Detailed scorecards are not public. |

## Search-strategy notes

- Search company name plus the source name. For example: `"siemens" site:glassdoor.com`.
- For non-English country sources, use a few keywords in the local language. Most modern registers have English interfaces.
- When the company has a clear parent in a different country, do separate searches for parent and branch entity.
- Skip aggregator sites that recycle the same data as the official register. Cite the primary source.
- For Norwegian companies, always check Miljøfyrtårn certification status as a baseline. Treat absence as neutral, not negative.
- jobbi.no is new (2025). Use only when at least three independent reviews point in the same direction. Never score a dimension on a single jobbi.no review.
- ISO certifications should be verified against the certifying body's register or a recent annual report mention, not just a self-claim on the company's About page.
- For GDPR posture, run a name search against both the home-country DPA enforcement page and enforcementtracker.com. Low threshold for inclusion: any decision in the last 36 months is in scope.
- For the AI Maturity Posture section (extended tier), at least three independent signal categories must produce findings before a score is assigned. Otherwise mark applicable=false with an applicability_note.
